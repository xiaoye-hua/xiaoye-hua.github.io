omdat

because

/ɔmˈdɑt/conjunction

Word forms

  • invariable: omdat (no inflected forms)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Both syllables (om- and -dat) are closed by a single consonant, giving the vowels their short sounds /ɔ/ and /ɑ/.

voordatbefore (conjunction)nadatafter (conjunction)zodatso that

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /t/ of 'omdat' often voices to [d] before a following voiced stop (e.g., 'omdat با' or 'omdat bij' becomes [ɔmˈdɑdbɛi]), or links smoothly onto a following vowel like in 'omdat ik' [ɔmˈdɑtɪk].
