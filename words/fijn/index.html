fijn

nice / pleasant

/ˈfɛinə/adjective

Word forms

  • inflected: fijne (fine/nice)
  • comparative: fijner (finer/nicer)
  • superlative: fijnst (finest/nicest)
  • partitive: fijns (something nice)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'ij' represents the diphthong /ɛi/, and the inflected form 'fijne' adds an unstressed schwa /-ə/ at the end.

wijnwinemijnminepijnpain

In connected speech

In the inflected form 'fijne', the final schwa /ə/ often elides when followed by a word starting with a vowel, such as in 'fijne avond' [ˈfɛin ˈaːvɔnt].
