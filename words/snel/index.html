snel

quickly

/snɛl/adverb

Word forms

  • comparative: sneller (faster)
  • superlative: snelst (fastest)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'sn' cluster is pronounced with a quick transition, and the 'e' is a short vowel.

kneljam, squeezespelgamestelset, couple

In connected speech

In fast speech, the final /l/ in ‘snel’ may become a 'dark l' [ɫ] or be slightly vocalized.
