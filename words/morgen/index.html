morgen

tomorrow

/ˈmɔrɣə(n)/adverb

Word forms

  • plural: morgens (mornings)
  • diminutive: morgentje (little morning)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The /r/ is pronounced with a uvular fricative /ɣ/ in the standard Dutch accent.

In connected speech

The final /n/ may be elided in casual speech, leading to a pronunciation like [ˈmɔrɣə].
