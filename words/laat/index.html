laat

last / final

/ˈlaːtstə/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: later (later)
  • superlative: laatst (latest)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The long vowel /aː/ is represented by 'aa' in an open syllable.

In connected speech

In fast speech, final /t/ in 'laat maar' can be softened or elided, making it sound like 'laa maar'.
