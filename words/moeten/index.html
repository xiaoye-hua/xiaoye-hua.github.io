moeten

must

/mut/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'oe' consistently represents the close back rounded vowel /u/ (like 'oo' in English 'boot'), unaffected by open or closed syllables.

voetenfeetboetento pay for / atonegroetento greet

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /-n/ of 'moeten' is typically dropped, and before a voiced stop (as in 'moet dat'), the /t/ in 'moet' assimilates to [d], sounding like [mudɑt].
