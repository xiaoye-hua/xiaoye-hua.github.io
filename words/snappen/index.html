snappen

gets / understands (informal)

/snɑpt/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Double consonant 'pp' in 'snappen' keeps the preceding 'a' vowel short /ɑ/ in an open syllable; in the closed syllable 'snap' or 'snapt', only one 'p' is needed to keep the vowel short.

In connected speech

In the question 'Snap je?' (Do you understand?), the /p/ and /j/ often coalesce in rapid speech, sounding close to [ˈsnɑpjə] or even [ˈsnɑpʃə] in some dialects.
