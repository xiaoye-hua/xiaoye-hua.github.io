jurk

dress

/jʏrk/noun

Word forms

  • gender: de-word (feminine/masculine)
  • plural: jurken (dresses)
  • diminutive: jurkje (little dress)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

In a closed syllable, 'u' represents the short rounded vowel /ʏ/. A fleeting schwa (svarabhakti vowel) is often inserted between /r/ and the final /k/, making it sound like 'jurrek'.

kurkcorkschurkvillainsnurksnore

In connected speech

When followed by a voiced stop like /b/ or /d/ (e.g., 'jurk dragen'), the final /k/ undergoes regressive voicing assimilation and is pronounced as [g].
