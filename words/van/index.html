/vɑn/preposition

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The short vowel /ɑ/ is common in monosyllabic words ending in -n.

manmankancan (noun)sansand

In connected speech

The final /n/ in ‘van’ is often elided in casual speech, particularly before consonants.
