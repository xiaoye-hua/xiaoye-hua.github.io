ingewikkeld

complicated

/ˈɪŋəˌʋɪkəlt/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: ingewikkelder (more complicated)
  • superlative: ingewikkeldst (most complicated)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'in-' indicates a state of being, and 'gewikkeld' comes from 'wikkelen' meaning to wrap, creating a sense of complexity.

onhandigawkwardgecompliceerdcomplexmoeilijkdifficult

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the 't' at the end of 'ingewikkeld' may be unreleased or assimilated into the following word, especially if it starts with a consonant.
