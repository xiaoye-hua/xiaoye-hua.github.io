met

with

/mɛt/preposition

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The vowel ‘e’ in ‘met’ is pronounced as a short open-mid front unrounded vowel /ɛ/.

netnetzetputspetcap

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /t/ in ‘met’ may be assimilated or become a glottal stop, especially before a word starting with a vowel.
