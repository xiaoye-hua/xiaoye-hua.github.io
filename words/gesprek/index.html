gesprek

conversation

/ɣəˈsprɛk/noun

Word forms

  • plural: gesprekken (conversations)
  • diminutive: gesprekje (little conversation)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'ge-' followed by 's' leads to a voiceless /xə/ sound, and 'spr' as an onset cluster maintains its pronounced consonants.

geschenkgiftgezichtfacegeestspirit

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /k/ in ‘gesprek’ can be unreleased or assimilated, especially before a vowel in the following word.
