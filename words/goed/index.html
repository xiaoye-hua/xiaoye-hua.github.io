goed

good

/ɣut/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: beter (better)
  • superlative: best (best)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Dutch /ɣ/ at the onset is a voiced velar fricative, typical in words beginning with 'g'.

grootbiggeelyellowgevento give

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /t/ in 'goed' can be unreleased or assimilate, especially before a vowel.
