zij

she

/zə/pronoun

Word forms

  • subject: zij (they)
  • object: hen/hun (them/their)
  • possessive: hun (their)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diphthong 'ij' represents the sound /ɛi/.

wijweblijhappyvrijfree

In connected speech

In connected speech, the initial /z/ can be voiced to a softer [z̥] when followed by a voiceless consonant.
