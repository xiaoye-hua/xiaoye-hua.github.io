verschrikkelijk

awful / terrible

/vərˈsxrɪkələk/adjective

Word forms

  • inflected: verschrikkelijke (terrible)
  • comparative: verschrikkelijker (more terrible)
  • superlative: verschrikkelijkst (most terrible)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The unstressed suffix '-lijk' is pronounced with a schwa as /lək/, not with a long 'ij' sound, and the double 'kk' keeps the preceding 'i' short /ɪ/.

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the unstressed first syllable 'ver-' reduces to [vər] or [fər], and the middle schwa can be dropped entirely, sounding like [fəˈsxrɪklək].
