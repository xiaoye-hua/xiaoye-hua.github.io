gevoel

feeling

/ɣəˈvul/noun

Word forms

  • article: het gevoel (neuter)
  • plural: gevoelens (feelings)
  • diminutive: gevoeletje (little feeling)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The unstressed prefix 'ge-' has a reduced schwa vowel /ə/, while the digraph 'oe' represents the high back vowel /u/ (like English 'oo' in 'boot').

voelento feeldoelgoal / targetstoelchair

In connected speech

In connected speech after a voiceless consonant, such as in 'het gevoel', the voiced fricative /v/ often devoiced and assimilates to a voiceless [f], sounding like [ət xəˈful].
