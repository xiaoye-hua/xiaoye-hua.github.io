/ɦu/adverb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The vowel sound /uː/ in 'hoe' is represented by the letters 'oe', a common Dutch spelling for this sound.

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /ˈɦuː/ in ‘hoe gaat’ may blend, with the /ɦ/ becoming less pronounced, sounding like /uːɣaːt/.
