romantisch

romantic

/roːˈmɑntisə/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: romantischer (more romantic)
  • superlative: meest romantisch (most romantic)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'ch' at the end is pronounced as /s/ due to Germanic influence.

fantastischfantasticcomischcomicklassiekclassical

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /s/ in ‘romantisch’ may become voiced due to assimilation with a following voiced sound, sounding like [z].
