/dɑt/conjunction

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Short vowel /ɑ/ in a closed syllable is represented by a single 'a'.

In connected speech

In connected speech, the /d/ in ‘dat is’ may be voiced to a soft [d̪] before the vowel.
