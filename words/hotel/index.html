hotel

/ɦoːˈtɛl/noun

Word forms

  • article: het hotel (the hotel)
  • plural: hotels (hotels)
  • diminutive: hotelletje (little hotel)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable 'ho-' has a long /oː/ sound, while the closed stressed syllable '-tel' has a short /ɛ/ sound, which requires doubling the 'l' in the diminutive (hotelletje) to keep the vowel short.

In connected speech

In rapid connected speech, the initial /ɦ/ is often dropped after a consonant (e.g., 'in het hotel' sounds like [ɪn ət oːˈtɛl]), and the final /l/ is pronounced as a 'dark l' [ɫ].
