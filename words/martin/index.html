martin

Martin

/ˈmɑr.tɪn/proper noun

Word forms

  • plural: martins (martins)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The /ɑ/ in the first syllable is a short vowel due to the closed syllable pattern.

karina given namegarmina brand namestartento start

In connected speech

In connected speech, the /n/ at the end of ‘martin’ may assimilate to a nasalized vowel before a following vowel sound.
