benieuwd

curious

/bəˈniʋt/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: benieuwder (more curious)
  • superlative: benieuwdst (most curious)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'uw' in 'benieuwd' is pronounced as /y/ followed by /t/ at the end.

nieuwnewschuwshytrouwloyal

In connected speech

The final /t/ in 'benieuwd' may be softened or dropped in rapid speech when the next word starts with a consonant.
