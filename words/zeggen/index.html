say

/ˈzɛɣə(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The ‘z’ at the start is pronounced /z/, a voiced sound, typical for words starting with ‘z’ in Dutch.

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the final /n/ in ‘zeggen we’ may become nasalized and blend into the following word.
