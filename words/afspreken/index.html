afspreken

meet up

/ˈɑf.spreː.kə(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'af-' is unstressed and pronounced /ɑf/, while the stem 'spreken' stresses the long vowel /eː/.

aansprekento addressuitsprekento pronouncetoesprekento speak to

In connected speech

In fast speech, the final /k/ in ‘afspreek ik’ may assimilate to a softer [k] or [g] sound.
