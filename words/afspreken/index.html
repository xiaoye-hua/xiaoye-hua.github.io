afspreken

meet up

/ˈɑf.spreː.kə(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

In open syllables like 'spre-ken' and 'af-ge-spro-ken', a single vowel letter represents a long vowel sound (/eː/ and /oː/). In closed syllables like 'spreek', the vowel must be doubled to maintain the long sound.

In connected speech

In natural speech, the final '-n' of 'afgesproken' is usually dropped, and the '/f/' in 'af-' may voice slightly to '[v]' before thevoiced '/ɣ/' (in Southern/Belgian Dutch), sounding like [ˈɑfχəsproːkə].
