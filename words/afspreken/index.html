afspreken

meet up

/ˈɑf.spreː.kə(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable 'spre-' in 'afspreken' has a long /eː/ written with a single 'e', which must be doubled to 'ee' in closed syllables like 'afspreekt' to preserve the long vowel sound.

brekento breakstekento stab / stingprekento preach

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /-n/ of 'afspreken' is usually dropped, and the /f/ may become slightly voiced to [v] before voiced consonants.
