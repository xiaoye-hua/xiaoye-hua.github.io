beduusd

bewildered

/bəˈdyst/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: beduusder (more dazed)
  • superlative: het beduusdst (most dazed)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'be-' is unstressed and pronounced as /bə/, while the vowel 'uu' is pronounced as a long /yː/.

bedoeldintendedbehoorlijkconsiderablebewustconscious

In connected speech

In connected speech, the initial /bə/ can be reduced to a schwa /ə/, making it sound like /ədyst/.
