hard

out loud / hard

/ɦɑrt/adverb

Word forms

  • inflected: harde (hard)
  • comparative: harder (harder/louder/faster)
  • superlative: hardst (hardest/loudest/fastest)
  • partitive: hards (something hard)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Final 'd' undergoes final devoicing to be pronounced as /t/, while returns to /d/ when inflected in 'harde' (/ˈɦɑr.də/).

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /t/ of 'hard' may become voiced [d] again before a voiced plosive like /b/ or /d/ (e.g., 'hard werken' or 'hard denken'), or link smoothly to a following vowel as [t].
