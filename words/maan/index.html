maan

moon

/maːn/noun

Word forms

  • plural: manen (moons)
  • diminutive: maantje (little moon)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The long vowel /aː/ is indicated by the doubled 'aa' in an open syllable, followed by a nasal /n/.

kraancranestaalsteelbaanjob

In connected speech

In connected speech, the /n/ in ‘maan’ may become more nasalized or even drop before a consonant, as in ‘de maan schijnt’.
