deze

this

/ˈdeːzə/determiner

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'ee' in 'deze' is pronounced as a long /eː/ sound.

mezemeze (a type of dish)lezento readbezembroom

In connected speech

In casual speech, the final 'e' may be reduced to a schwa sound, sounding like /ˈdeːzə/.
