toch

too

/tɔx/adverb

Word forms

  • no plural or diminutive forms

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The ch sound is represented by /x/, a voiceless velar fricative.

grachtcanallichtlightnachtnight

In connected speech

In connected speech, the /x/ in ‘toch’ can become softer, especially between vowels.
