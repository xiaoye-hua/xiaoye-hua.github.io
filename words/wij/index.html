wij

we

/ʋə/pronoun

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diphthong /ɛi/ is commonly represented by the letters 'ij' in Dutch.

zijtheyblijhappyvrijfree

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the diphthong /ɛi/ can be reduced, leading 'wij zijn' to sound like /ʋə zɛin/.
