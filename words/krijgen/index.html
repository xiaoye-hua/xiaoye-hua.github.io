How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diphthong /ɛi/ in ‘krijg’ is common in Dutch, representing the ‘ij’ spelling.

rijdento driveblijvento staywijzento point

In connected speech

In rapid speech, final /ɣ/ in ‘krijg ik’ may be softened or elided, merging into [ɣ] or [k̚].
