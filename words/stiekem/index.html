stiekem

secretly / on the sly

/ˈstikəm/adverb

Word forms

  • inflected: stiekeme (secret/sneaky)
  • comparative: stiekemer (more secretive)
  • superlative: stiekemst (most secretive)
  • noun: stiekemerd (sneaky person)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'ie' represents the close front unrounded vowel /i/, while the unstressed 'e' in the final syllable '-em' is reduced to a schwa /ə/ (making it a common spelling mistake, as people often write 'stiekum').

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the final /m/ in 'stiekem' may partially assimilate to a labiodental [ɱ] when followed by a word starting with /f/ or /v/, such as in 'stiekem verliefd'.
