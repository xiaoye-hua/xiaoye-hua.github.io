tent

/tɛnt/noun

Word forms

  • gender: de-word (common)
  • plural: tenten (tents)
  • diminutive: tentje (little tent / casual bar or restaurant)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

A single 'e' in a closed syllable (ending in the consonant cluster -nt) represents the short vowel /ɛ/.

centcentventguy / fellowkrentcurrant

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the final /t/ is often dropped when followed by a word starting with a consonant, making 'een tent bouwen' sound like /ən ˈtɛn ˈbʌu.ə(n)/.
