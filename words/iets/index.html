iets

something

/its/pronoun

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diphthong 'ie' is pronounced as /i/, followed by a voiceless /ts/ sound.

nietsnothingietsjea little somethingiemandsomeone

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the final /ts/ in 'iets' may be pronounced as a softer [ts] or even [t] when followed by a vowel.
