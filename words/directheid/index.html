directheid

directness

/diˈrɛktɦɛit/noun

Word forms

  • gender: de-word (feminine)
  • plural: directheden (rarely used, uncountable)
  • diminutive: none

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The suffix '-heid' contains the diphthong 'ei' /ɛi/, and the final 'd' undergoes final devoicing to be pronounced as /t/.

snelheidspeedeerlijkheidhonestyvrijheidfreedom

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /ɦ/ in '-heid' often drops after the voiceless /t/, merging the middle cluster so it sounds like [diˈrɛktɛit].
