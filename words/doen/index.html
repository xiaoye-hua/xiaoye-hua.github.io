/dun/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The spelling ‘oe’ consistently represents the long /u/ sound.

In connected speech

In fast speech, final /n/ in ‘doen’ often becomes nasalized or drops before a vowel, as in ‘doe ik’.
