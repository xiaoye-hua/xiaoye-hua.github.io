oeh

ooh (expresses surprise, excitement, or mild hesitation)

/u/interjection

Word forms

  • invariable: oeh (does not inflect)
  • diminutive (as noun): oehje (little 'ooh' sound)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'oe' represents the close back rounded vowel /u/ (like 'oo' in English 'boot'), while the final 'h' is silent and merely indicates a lengthened or breathy exclamation.

koecowpoehphew / poohboehboo

In connected speech

In expressive speech, the vowel is often significantly lengthened to [uː] and may end with a slight breathy release [h] or glottal stop [ʔ] depending on the speaker's emotion.
