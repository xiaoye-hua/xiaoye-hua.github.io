advies

advice

/ɑtˈvis/noun

Word forms

  • plural: adviezen (advices)
  • diminutive: adviesje (little advice)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'd' in 'advies' is pronounced /d/ at the start of the word, and the 'v' is pronounced as /v/.

advocaatlawyeradverterento advertiseadventadvent

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the final /s/ in 'advies' may be softened or elided before a following vowel.
