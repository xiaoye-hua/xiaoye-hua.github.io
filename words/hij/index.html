hij

he

/ɦɛi̯/pronoun

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diphthong 'ij' is pronounced as /ɛi̯/, commonly found in Dutch words.

mijmezijthey/shewijwe

In connected speech

In connected speech, 'hij is' often reduces to /ɦɛis/ with the initial /ɦ/ sometimes dropping.
