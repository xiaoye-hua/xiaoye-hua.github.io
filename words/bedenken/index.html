bedenken

comes up with / thinks of

/bəˈdɛŋkt/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The letter 'n' before 'k' is pronounced as the velar nasal /ŋ/ (like 'ng' in 'sing'), while the 'e' in the unstressed prefix 'be-' is reduced to a schwa /ə/.

denkento thinkbedankento thankschenkento pour / to donate

In connected speech

In rapid connected speech, the final /t/ in 'bedenkt' is often dropped when followed by a word starting with a consonant (e.g., 'hij bedenkt zich' sounds like 'hij bedenk zich').
