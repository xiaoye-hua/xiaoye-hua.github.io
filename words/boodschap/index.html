boodschap

groceries / grocery shopping

/ˈboːtsxɑpə(n)/noun

Word forms

  • singular: de boodschap (message, errand)
  • plural: de boodschappen (messages, groceries)
  • diminutive: het boodschapje (little message/errand)
  • diminutive plural: de boodschapjes (little messages/groceries)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'd' at the end of the first syllable 'bood-' undergoes final devoicing to /t/ before the voiceless consonant cluster 'sch' (/sx/). In the plural 'boodschappen', the short vowel /ɑ/ requires the 'p' to be doubled to keep the syllable closed.

In connected speech

In the common phrase 'boodschappen doen' (to do groceries), the final /n/ of 'boodschappen' is usually dropped in natural speech, resulting in [ˈboːtsxɑpə ˈdun].
