vriend

friend

/vrint/noun

Word forms

  • plural: vrienden (friends)
  • diminutive: vriendje (little friend)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The initial cluster 'vr-' is common in Dutch, and the final '-d' is pronounced as /t/.

vriesfreezevreemdstrangevrijfree

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the final /t/ in 'vriend' may be less pronounced or even dropped when followed by a word starting with a consonant.
