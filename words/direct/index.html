direct

direct / straightforward

/diˈrɛkt/adjective

Word forms

  • inflected: directe (direct)
  • comparative: directer (more direct)
  • superlative: directst (most direct)
  • related noun: directie (management / board)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

In loanwords of Latin/French origin, the letter 'c' before 't' is pronounced as /k/, and the unstressed 'i' in an open syllable retains a tense /i/ sound rather than a long /iː/ or short /ɪ/.

In connected speech

In rapid connected speech, the final /t/ in the /kt/ cluster is frequently dropped before a word starting with a consonant, making 'direct naar' sound like [diˈrɛk naːr].
