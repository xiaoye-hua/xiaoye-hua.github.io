gewoon

ordinary

/ɣəˈʋoːnə/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: gewoner (more ordinary)
  • superlative: gewoonst (most ordinary)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix ge- is unstressed, leading to a reduced /ə/ sound, while -woon carries the main stress and is pronounced /ʋoːn/.

bewooninhabitedverwoonaccustomedgewoontehabit

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the initial /ɣə/ may reduce to a light /ə/ sound, leading to a pronunciation closer to /əˈʋoːn/.
