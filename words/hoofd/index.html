hoofd

head (uit je hoofd = by heart)

/ɦoːft/noun

Word forms

  • plural: hoofden (heads)
  • diminutive: hoofdje (little head)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The vowel 'oo' is pronounced as /oː/ in open syllables, but the final 'd' is devoiced to /t/ in the singular form.

roodredgrootbigbroodbread

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the final /t/ in 'hoofd' can be unreleased or assimilated, especially before a word starting with a consonant.
