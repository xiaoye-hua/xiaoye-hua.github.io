well

/ʋɛl/adverb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The short vowel /ɛ/ is pronounced because the syllable is closed by the consonant /l/.

velskintelcountbelbell

In connected speech

In connected speech, the /l/ in ‘wel ik’ might become a dark [ɫ] before a vowel.
