kus

kiss

/kʏs/noun

Word forms

  • singular: de kus (the kiss)
  • plural: kussen (kisses)
  • diminutive: kusje (little kiss)
  • related verb: kussen (to kiss)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

A single 'u' in a closed syllable (ending in a consonant) is pronounced as the short vowel /ʏ/. To keep this vowel short when adding the plural ending '-en', the final consonant 's' is doubled to 'ss' (kussen).

busbuszussistermussparrow

In connected speech

In the common diminutive 'kusje', the /s/ and /j/ typically coalesce into a postalveolar fricative [ʃ], making it sound like [ˈkʏʃə].
