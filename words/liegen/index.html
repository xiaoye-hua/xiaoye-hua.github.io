liegen

lie (infinitive at the end of the clause, governed by 'hoeft ... te')

/ˈliɣə(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'ie' consistently represents the high front vowel /i/, while the 'g' is a velar/uvular fricative /ɣ/ between vowels and devoiced to /x/ at the end of a syllable (as in 'lieg').

vliegento flybedriegento deceivewiegento rock / cradle

In connected speech

In natural speech, the final -n of 'liegen' is usually dropped, and in 'lieg ik', the final devoiced /x/ of 'lieg' links directly to the vowel of 'ik' as [li.xɪk].
