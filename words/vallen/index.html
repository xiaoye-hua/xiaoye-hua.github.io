vallen

am into (vallen op = be attracted to)

/vɑl/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The double consonant 'll' in 'vallen' keeps the preceding vowel 'a' short (/ɑ/). In a closed syllable like 'val' or 'valt', only one 'l' is needed to keep the vowel short.

ballenballsknallento pop / bangstallenstables / to park

In connected speech

In connected speech, the initial /v/ of 'valt' often devoiced to [f] after voiceless consonants (e.g., 'het valt' sounds like [ət fɑlt]), and the final -n of 'vallen' is usually dropped.
