lelijk

ugly

/ˈleːləkə/adjective

Word forms

  • base: lelijk (ugly)
  • inflected: lelijke (ugly)
  • comparative: lelijker (uglier)
  • superlative: lelijkst (ugliest)
  • partitive: lelijks (something ugly)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable 'le-' represents a long /eː/ sound, while the unstressed suffix '-lijk' is reduced to /lək/ with a schwa rather than the full /ɛi/ diphthong.

In connected speech

In the inflected form 'lelijke', the final schwa /ə/ is frequently elided in connected speech when followed by a noun starting with a vowel (e.g., 'lelijke eend' sounds like [ˈleːlək ˈeːnt]).
