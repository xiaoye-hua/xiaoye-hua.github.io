nederlands

Dutch

/ˈneːdərˌlɑnts/noun

Word forms

  • comparative: Nederlandser (more Dutch)
  • superlative: Nederlandst (most Dutch)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'ee' in 'nee' is a long vowel /eː/ and the 'a' in 'lands' is a short vowel /ɑ/.
