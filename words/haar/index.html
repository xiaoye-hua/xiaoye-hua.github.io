haar

her

/ɦaːr/possessive pronoun

Word forms

  • plural: haren (hairs)
  • diminutive: haartje (little hair)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable 'haa-' indicates a long /aː/ sound.

paarpairwaarwherejaaryear

In connected speech

In connected speech, the initial /ɦ/ in 'haar' may become a voiced glottal fricative or even be dropped entirely in fast speech.
