nog

still

/nɔx/adverb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The word 'nog' ends in a voiced velar fricative /x/, which is common in Dutch adverbs.

dagdayvlagflagwegway

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the final /x/ in ‘nog’ may be devoiced or slightly reduced before a vowel.
