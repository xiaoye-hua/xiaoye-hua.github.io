/ˈtuɣeːvə(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'toe-' indicates an action towards something, affecting the verb's meaning.

toelatento allowtoekennento granttoestemmento consent

In connected speech

In fast speech, the /t/ in 'geef toe' may assimilate to [d] when following a voiced sound.
