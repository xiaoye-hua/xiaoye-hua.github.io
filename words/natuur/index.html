natuur

nature

/naːˈtyːr/noun

Word forms

  • plural: naturen (natures)
  • diminutive: natuurje (little nature)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The vowel sound /aː/ is long due to the open syllable structure, and /yːr/ is long due to the final 'r'.

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /r/ in 'natuur' may be pronounced more softly or even dropped, especially in casual conversation.
