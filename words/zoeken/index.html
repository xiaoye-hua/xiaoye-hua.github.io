looking

/zuk/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The oo in zoek- represents the long vowel /oː/, shortened in closed syllables.

In connected speech

Final /k/ in ‘zoek ik’ often assimilates to an unreleased [k̚] before the following vowel.
