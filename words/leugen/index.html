leugen

little lie / white lie

/ˈløːɣəɲtʃə/noun

Word forms

  • article: de leugen (the lie)
  • plural: leugens (lies)
  • diminutive: leugentje (little lie)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'eu' represents the front rounded vowel /øː/, while the 'g' before an unstressed 'en' is pronounced as a voiced velar fricative /ɣ/ (or voiceless /x/ in northern accents), and the final 'n' is often dropped.

keukenkitchenheugento rememberdeugento be good/virtuous

In connected speech

In natural connected speech, the final /-n/ is usually dropped, unless the next word starts with a vowel (e.g., 'leugen is'), where it may be retained as a linking consonant [n].
