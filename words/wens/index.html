wens

wishes

/ˈʋɛnsə(n)/noun

Word forms

  • plural: wensen (wishes)
  • diminutive: wensje (little wish)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'e' in 'wens' represents the short /ɛ/ sound, typical in closed syllables.

menshumanpensbellylenslens

In connected speech

In fast speech, the final /s/ in 'wens ik' might be pronounced as a softer [z] when followed by a voiced sound.
