tegen

against

/ˈteːɣə(n)/preposition

Word forms

  • conjunction: tegen dat (against that)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The sequence 'ee' in an open syllable is pronounced as long /eː/.

In connected speech

The /ɣ/ in ‘tegen’ can be pronounced as a voiced velar fricative or a softer voiced palatal fricative depending on regional accents.
