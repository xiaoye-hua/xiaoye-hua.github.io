koffer

suitcase

/ˈkɔfər/noun

Word forms

  • plural: koffers (suitcases)
  • diminutive: koffertje (little suitcase)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The closed syllable 'kof' contains a short /ɔ/ sound, while the open syllable 'fer' ends with a schwa /ər/.

offerteofferkofferbaktrunk (of a car)stofferbroom

In connected speech

The final /r/ in ‘koffer’ may be less pronounced or even dropped in casual speech, often sounding more like [ˈkɔ.fə].
