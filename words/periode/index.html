periode

period

/ˌpeːriˈjoːdə/noun

Word forms

  • plural: periodes (periods)
  • diminutive: periodeje (little period)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'e' at the end of 'periode' is pronounced as /ə/, a schwa sound commonly used in unstressed syllables.

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /ə/ in 'periode' may be reduced further or elided, especially in fast speech.
