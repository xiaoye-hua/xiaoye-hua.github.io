vanuit

from (out of / from inside)

/vɑnˈœyt/preposition

Word forms

  • invariable: vanuit (no inflection)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'ui' represents the front rounded diphthong /œy/, and the 'a' in the unstressed first syllable is pronounced as a short /ɑ/.

vooruitforward / aheadachteruitbackwardbuitenoutside

In connected speech

In connected speech, the medial /n/ smoothly resyllabifies to the onset of the stressed second syllable, sounding like [vɑ.ˈnœyt], and the final /t/ may become voiced [d] before a word starting with a voiced stop.
