/mɛin/possessive pronoun

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diphthong /ɛi/ is spelled as 'ij' in Dutch, which is pronounced similarly to 'ei'.

In connected speech

In connected speech, the diphthong /ɛi/ in ‘mijn’ might be pronounced more smoothly, closer to [mɛi̯n].
