How it is spelled and how it sounds

In French loanwords, the letter combination -cia- often coalesces into a palatal /ʃaː/ or /sjaː/ sound, while the double 'aa' in the closed final syllable reduces to a single 'a' in the open syllable of the inflected form (speciaal -> speciale).
