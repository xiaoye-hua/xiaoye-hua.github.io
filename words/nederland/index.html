nederland

Netherlands

/ˈneːdərlɑnt/proper noun

Word forms

  • plural: Nederlanden (Netherlands as multiple regions)
  • diminutive: Nederlandje (little Netherlands)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable 'nee-' indicates a long /eː/, while '-der-' and '-land' reflect standard Dutch vowel and consonant patterns.
