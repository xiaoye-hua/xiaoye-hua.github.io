kunnen

can

/ˈkʏnə(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'u' in 'kunnen' is pronounced as a short /ʏ/, typical for closed syllables.

gunnento grantmogenmay, to be allowed tozullenshall, will

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /n/ in 'kun je' might be elided, resulting in /kʏ jə/.
