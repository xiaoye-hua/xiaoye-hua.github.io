pauze

break

/ˈpʌu̯zə/noun

Word forms

  • article: de pauze (common gender)
  • plural: pauzes (breaks)
  • diminutive: pauzetje (little break)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'au' represents the diphthong /ʌu/ (pronounced identically to 'ou'), and the final unstressed 'e' represents a schwa /ə/.

autocarblauwbluesaussauce

In connected speech

In casual speech, the final schwa /ə/ in 'pauze' often elides when followed by a word starting with a vowel, such as in 'pauze houden' [ˈpʌu.zɦʌu.də(n)].
