overheersen

takes over / dominates / prevails

/ˌoːvərˈɦeːrst/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Double 'ee' before 'r' (/eːr/) creates a centering diphthong-like sound [ɪːr] in standard Dutch, while the unstressed 'er' in 'over' reduces to a schwa /ər/.

heersento rule / prevailbeheersento master / controloverwegento consider (also inseparable 'over-')

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final '-t' in 'overheerst' is often dropped when followed by a word starting with a consonant (e.g., 'overheerst door' sounds like [oːvərˈɦeːrs doːr]).
