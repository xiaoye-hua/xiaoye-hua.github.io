woensdag

Wednesday

/ˈʋoːnsˌdaːx/noun

Word forms

  • plural: woensdagen (Wednesdays)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'oe' in woensdag indicates a long /u/ sound, typical for Dutch words.

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /d/ in 'woensdag' may become a flap or even be omitted, sounding like 'woensag'.
