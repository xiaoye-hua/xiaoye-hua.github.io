zeker

certainly

/ˈzeːkər/adverb

Word forms

  • comparative: zekerder (more certain)
  • superlative: zekerst (most certain)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The initial 'z' is pronounced as /z/ and the 'e' is long /eː/ in open syllables.

zegenblessingzevensevenzeervery

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the final /r/ in 'zeker' may be realized as a tap or even omitted, blending into the following word.
