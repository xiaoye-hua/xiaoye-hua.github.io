etentje

dinner

/ˈeː.tən.cə/noun

Word forms

  • plural: etentjes (small meals)
  • diminutive: etentje (small meal)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diminutive suffix -tje indicates a small or endearing version; the '-e' before it is common in Dutch.

huisjelittle houseboompjelittle treekatjekitten

In connected speech

The /j/ sound in '-tje' can be softened or even omitted in rapid speech, making it sound like 'etent-e'.
