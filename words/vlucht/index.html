vlucht

flight

/vlʏxt/noun

Word forms

  • plural: vluchten (flights)
  • diminutive: vluchtje (little flight)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The cluster 'vl' followed by 'ucht' represents the /vlʏxt/ sound with a short vowel.

luchtairzuchtsighvruchtfruit

In connected speech

In casual speech, the final /t/ in 'vlucht' may be less aspirated or even dropped before a following consonant.
