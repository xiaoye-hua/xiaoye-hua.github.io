Amsterdam

/ˌɑmstərˈdɑm/proper noun

Word forms

  • plural: Amsterdams (not commonly used)
  • diminutive: Amsterdammertje (little Amsterdam)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The initial /ɑm/ is pronounced with a short 'a', and the 'st' cluster is pronounced together.
