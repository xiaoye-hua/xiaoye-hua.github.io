bezoek

visit

/bəˈzuk/noun

Word forms

  • plural: bezoeken (visits)
  • diminutive: bezoekje (little visit)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'be-' often signifies an action or process, and the 'oe' spelling represents the /u/ sound.

verzoekrequestbehoefteneedberoepprofession

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /k/ in ‘bezoek ik’ may become less aspirated, sounding closer to [ɡ] before a vowel.
