helemaal

at all

/ˌheː.ləˈmaːl/adverb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable ‘hee-’ has a long /eː/ sound, while the closed syllable ‘-maal’ has a long /aː/ sound.

In connected speech

In connected speech, the /l/ in ‘helemaal’ may become more like a flap or be elided, especially before vowels.
