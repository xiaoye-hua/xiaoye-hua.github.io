vroeg

early

/ˈvruːɣə/adjective

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'v' is voiced /v/ followed by a long /uː/ and guttural /x/.

vroegerearliervruchtfruitvrijfree

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /x/ in 'vroeg hij' may become a softer /ɦ/ before the vowel.
