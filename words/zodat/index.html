zodat

so that

/zoːˈdɑt/conjunction

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable 'zo-' indicates a long /oː/ sound.

zoveras far aszowelas well aszoveelso much

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /t/ in ‘zodat ik’ may become a flap or even be dropped before a vowel.
