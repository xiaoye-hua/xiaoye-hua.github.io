jij

you

/jə/pronoun

Word forms

  • emphatic: jij
  • unstressed: je

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diphthong /ɛi/ is spelled as 'ij', common in Dutch pronouns.

zijshe/theywijwemijme

In connected speech

In rapid speech, ‘jij’ can be reduced to a less stressed form ‘je’.
