augustus

August

/ʌu̯ˈɣʏstʏs/noun

Word forms

  • plural: augustusmaanden (Augusts)
  • diminutive: augustusje (little August)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The combination 'au' in Dutch is pronounced as /ʌu/, and 'g' before 'u' is pronounced as /x/.

In connected speech

In fast speech, the /s/ in ‘augustus is’ may be pronounced as a [z] due to assimilation with the following vowel.
