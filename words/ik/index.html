ik

I

/ɪk/pronoun

Word forms

  • subject (unemphatic/clitic): 'k (I)
  • object (stressed): mij (me)
  • object (unstressed): me (me)
  • possessive: mijn (my)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

A single 'i' in a closed syllable (ending in a consonant) always represents the short vowel /ɪ/.

dikthick / fatblikglance / tin canstiksuffocate / choke (stem)

In connected speech

In unstressed positions, the vowel often reduces to a schwa /ə/ (sometimes written as 'k), and the final /k/ voices to [ɡ] before voiced plosives like /b/ or /d/ (e.g., 'ik ben' sounds like [ɪɡ bɛn]).
