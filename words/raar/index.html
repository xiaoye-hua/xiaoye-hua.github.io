raar

strange / strangely / funny

/raːr/adverb

Word forms

  • inflected: rare (strange)
  • comparative: raarder (stranger)
  • superlative: raarst (strangest)
  • partitive: raars (something strange)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Double 'aa' represents a long /aː/ in the closed syllable 'raar', which becomes a single 'a' in the open syllable of the inflected form 'rare' (ra-re), while the comparative adds '-der' instead of '-er' because the stem ends in '-r'.

In connected speech

In standard Netherlandic Dutch, the final /r/ in 'raar' is often vocalized or pronounced as a retroflex approximant [ɻ], which links smoothly as an alveolar tap [ɾ] if the next word starts with a vowel (e.g., 'raar idee').
