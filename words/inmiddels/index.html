inmiddels

by now / meanwhile / in the meantime

/ɪnˈmɪdəls/adverb

Word forms

  • invariable: inmiddels (no inflected forms)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Double consonant 'dd' keeps the preceding 'i' short /ɪ/, while the unstressed 'e' in '-dels' reduces to a schwa /ə/.

middelmeans / middleinmiddelarchaic root of inmiddelsbiddendpraying

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /n/ in the prefix 'in-' often assimilates to the following bilabial /m/, making it sound like [ɪˈmɪdəls].
