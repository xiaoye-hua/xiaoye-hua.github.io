How it is spelled and how it sounds

Thevoiced /v/ in the infinitive 'overdrijven' devoicesto /f/ and is spelled with 'f' at the end of a syllable, as in the stem 'overdrijf' and singular past 'overdreef'.

In connected speech

In inversion like 'overdrijf je', the final /f/ and following /j/ often blend smoothly, while in 'overdrijf dat' the /f/ may become voiced [v] before the voiced /d/.
