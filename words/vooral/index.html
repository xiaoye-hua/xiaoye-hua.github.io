vooral

mainly

/voːˈrɑl/adverb

Word forms

  • invariable: vooral (no inflectional forms)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'voor-' contains a long 'oo' (/oː/) modified by the following 'r', while the stressed second syllable '-al' is a closed syllable with a short 'a' (/ɑ/).

overaleverywherevoorafbeforehandvoortaanfrom now on

In connected speech

In rapid connected speech, the unstressed first syllable /voːr/ often reduces to /vər/ or /fər/, making the word sound like [vəˈrɑl] or [fəˈrɑl].
