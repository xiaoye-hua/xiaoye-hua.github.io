hopelijk

hopefully

/ˈɦoːpələk/adverb

Word forms

  • base form: hopelijk (invariable as an adverb)
  • inflected adjective form: hopelijke (hopeful, rare)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable 'ho-' represents a long /oː/, while the unstressed suffix '-lijk' reduces its vowel from /ɛi/ to a schwa /ə/, pronounced as /-lək/.

mogelijkpossible / possiblyvrolijkcheerfulduidelijkclear / clearly

In connected speech

In rapid connected speech, the middle schwa in /-pə-/ is often elided, reducing the pronunciation to two syllables [ˈɦoːplək], and the final /k/ may become voiced [ɡ] before a voiced stop like /b/ or /d/.
