oktober

October

/ɔkˈtoːbər/noun

Word forms

  • plural: oktobers (Octobers)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The initial 'o' is pronounced /ɔ/ in stressed position, and the 'e' in the final syllable is pronounced /ər/ as a schwa.

novemberNovemberdecemberDecemberoktoberfeestOktoberfest

In connected speech

The final /r/ in ‘oktober’ may be pronounced as a schwa [ə] in casual speech, especially in Dutch dialects where the rhotic is reduced.
