schrijven

write / to write

/ˈsxrɛi̯.və(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The initial cluster 'schr-' is pronounced /sxr/, and the voiced /v/ in 'schrijven' devoices to /f/ at the end of a syllable ('schrijf').

blijvento staydrijvento float / to drivewrijvento rub

In connected speech

In inverted questions like 'schrijf je', the final /f/ and initial /j/ often blend into a palatalized [fj] or voiced [vj] in fast speech.
