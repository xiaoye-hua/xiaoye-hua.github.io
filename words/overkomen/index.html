overkomen

happens to / befalls

/ˌoːvərˈkɔmt/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Unlike regular verbs, 'komen' has a long /oː/ in the open syllable of the infinitive, but shortens to /ɔ/ in the closed syllables of the singular present ('overkom', 'overkomt').

komento comevoorkomento preventbekomento recover / to get

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /t/ of 'overkomt' is often dropped when followed by a word starting with a consonant, such as in 'wat overkomt mij' [ʋɑt oːvərˈkɔm mɛi].
