you (plural)

/ˈjʏ.li/pronoun

Word forms

  • possessive: jullie (your, plural)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The spelling 'ju-' indicates the /jʏ/ sound, and '-lie' indicates /li/.

julyulejurkdressjufteacher (female)

In connected speech

In rapid speech, 'jullie' might be pronounced with a reduced /jə/ instead of /jʏ/.
