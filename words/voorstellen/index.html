introduce

/ˈvoːrˌstɛlə(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'voor-' indicates the separable nature and changes pronunciation when separated.

voorspellento predictvoorkomento preventvoorbereidento prepare

In connected speech

In fast speech, 'stel' in 'stel ik voor' may reduce to [stəl] with a schwa.
