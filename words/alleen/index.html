alleen

only

/ɑˈleːn/adverb

Word forms

  • base form (invariable): alleen (alone / only)
  • related inflected adjective: allenig / allenige (all alone / solitary)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The double consonant 'll' ensures the preceding 'a' is pronounced short (/ɑ/), while the double 'ee' represents a long vowel (/eː/) in the stressed final syllable.

algemeengeneralgemeenmean / commonsteenstone

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the unstressed initial vowel /ɑ/ is often reduced to a schwa [ə] or dropped entirely, making it sound like [əˈleːn] or [ˈleːn].
