veel

a lot

/veːl/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: meer (more)
  • superlative: meest (most)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The vowel 'ee' in a closed syllable is pronounced as the long vowel /eː/.

heelwholedeelpartsteelstem

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /l/ in 'veel' may become a dark [ɫ], especially before a consonant.
