echt

really

/ɛxt/adverb

Word forms

  • inflected: echte (real)
  • comparative: echter (more genuine)
  • superlative: echtst (most genuine)
  • partitive: echts (something real)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

A single 'e' in a closed syllable represents the short vowel /ɛ/, and the digraph 'ch' represents the voiceless velar/uvular fricative /x/.

rechtstraight / rightslechtbadvechtfight (1sg present)

In connected speech

In fast, casual speech, the final /t/ is frequently dropped before a word starting with a consonant, making 'echt waar' sound like [ɛx ˈʋaːr].
