boos

angrily (describes how Annika says it)

/boːs/adverb (adjective used adverbially)

Word forms

  • inflected: boze (angry)
  • comparative: bozer (angrier)
  • superlative: boost / het booste (angriest)
  • partitive: boos (something angry, used after 'iets' or 'niets')

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Double 'oo' in a closed syllable represents the long vowel /oː/, which becomes a single 'o' in an open syllable (bo-ze) while the final /s/ voices to /z/.

In connected speech

In connected speech before a vowel-initial word, such as in 'boos op', the final /s/ often undergoes intervocalic voicing and resyllabification, sounding like [boː.zɔp].
