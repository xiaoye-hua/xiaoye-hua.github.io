weer

again

/ʋeːr/adverb

Word forms

  • plural: weer (weathers)
  • diminutive: weertje (little weather)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The long /eː/ sound is represented by 'ee', a common pattern in Dutch for long vowels.

meerlakeneerdownzeervery

In connected speech

In quick speech, the /ʋ/ in ‘weer het’ may be pronounced with less lip rounding, approaching a /v/ sound.
