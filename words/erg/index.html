erg

very

/ɛr(ə)x/adverb

Word forms

  • comparative: erger (worse)
  • superlative: ergst (worst)
  • adverbial: erg (very)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'g' in 'erg' is pronounced as a voiced velar fricative /ɣ/.

bergmountainergsteworstverfar

In connected speech

In fast speech, the final /ɣ/ in 'erg' may become less voiced or assimilate to a /k/ when followed by a voiceless consonant.
