ondertussen

meanwhile / in the meantime

/ˌɔndərˈtʏsə(n)/adverb

Word forms

  • invariable: ondertussen (no inflected forms)
  • synonym: intussen (meanwhile)
  • synonym: inmiddels (by now, meanwhile)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Double consonant 'ss' keeps the preceding vowel 'u' short (/ʏ/), while the unstressed endings '-er' and '-en' reduce to schwa (/ə/).

intussenmeanwhilekussenpillow / to kissonderzoekento investigate

In connected speech

In natural speech, the final '-n' is usually dropped, and the internal '/d/' following '/n/' may weaken, making it sound closer to [ˌɔnərˈtʏsə].
