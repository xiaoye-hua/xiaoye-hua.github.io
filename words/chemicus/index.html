chemicus

chemist

/ˈxeːmikʏs/noun

Word forms

  • plural: chemici (chemists)
  • diminutive: chemicusje (little chemist)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The initial 'ch' is pronounced as /x/ in Dutch, which is a voiceless velar fricative.

chroomchromechiquechicchaoschaos

In connected speech

The final /s/ in ‘chemicus’ may be pronounced as a voiced [z] in connected speech when followed by a voiced consonant.
