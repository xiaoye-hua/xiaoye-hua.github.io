Lars

/lɑrs/proper noun

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Short a /ɑ/ before r + consonant, as in 'hard' and 'warm'.

hardhard, loudwarmwarm

In connected speech

The r is often weak or approximant-like before the s in casual speech.
