Xing

/ksɪŋ/proper noun

Word forms

  • plural: xingen (crossings)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The letter 'x' typically represents the /ks/ sound in Dutch, followed by the short vowel /ɪ/.

taxitaxifixto fixboxbox

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /ks/ sound in 'xing' may be pronounced more smoothly, with less emphasis on the /k/ sound.
