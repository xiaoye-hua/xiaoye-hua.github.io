per

per / a (as in 'twice a day')

/pɛr/preposition

Word forms

  • invariable: per (no inflection)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

A single 'e' in a closed syllable represents the short vowel /ɛ/, though the following 'r' often colors the vowel slightly toward /ɛː/.

verfarsterstarherhere / time ago (in 'van oudsher')

In connected speech

In unstressed positions within rapid speech, such as 'per ongeluk' (by accident), the vowel in 'per' often reduces to a schwa [pər] and the final /r/ links directly to the following vowel.
