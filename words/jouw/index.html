jouw

your

/jə/possessive pronoun

Word forms

  • unstressed form: je (your)
  • independent possessive (de-words): de jouwe (yours)
  • independent possessive (het-words): het jouwe (yours)
  • object pronoun: jou (you)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The trigraph 'ouw' is pronounced as the diphthong /ɑu/, making 'jouw' (your) a homophone of 'jou' (you).

vrouwwoman / wifekoudcoldblauwblue

In connected speech

In natural, unstressed speech, 'jouw' is almost always replaced by its reduced counterpart 'je' (/jə/), unless specifically emphasizing ownership.
