lachen

to laugh / laughing

/ˈlɑxə(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'ch' represents the voiceless velar/uvular fricative /x/, and keeps the preceding vowel 'a' short /ɑ/, even without doubling the consonant.

kachelstove / heaterkuchento coughechoecho

In connected speech

In casual connected speech, the final '-en' is usually reduced to a schwa /ə/ (pronounced [ˈlɑxə]), and the /x/ may become voiced [ɣ] before a following voiced consonant.
