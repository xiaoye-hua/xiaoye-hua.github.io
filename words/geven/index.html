/ɣeːft/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Open syllable 'ge-' has a long /eː/ spelled with one 'e', while closed syllable 'geef' requires 'ee' to keep the vowel long, and final 'v' devoices to 'f'.

In connected speech

In natural speech, the final '-en' in 'geven' is usually reduced to a schwa /ˈɣeːvə/, and in 'geef ik', the /f/ may lightly voice to [v] before the vowel.
