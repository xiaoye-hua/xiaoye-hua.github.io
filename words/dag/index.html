dag

days

/ˈdaːɣə(n)/noun

Word forms

  • article: de dag (the day)
  • plural: dagen (days)
  • diminutive: dagje (little day / day trip)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

A single 'a' in a closed syllable represents the short vowel /ɑ/, and a final 'g' is devoiced to a voiceless velar/uvular fricative /x/.

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final voiceless /x/ in 'dag' often voices to [ɣ] when followed immediately by a word starting with a vowel, as in 'dag allemaal'.
