verliefd

in love

/vərˈlifd/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: verliefder (more in love)
  • superlative: verliefdst (most in love)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'ver-' is unstressed, and the vowel 'ie' in the stressed syllable is pronounced as /iː/.

In connected speech

The final /d/ in ‘verliefd op’ may become unreleased [d̚] or assimilate to [t] before voiceless consonants.
