namelijk

actually

/ˈnaːməˌlɪk/adverb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable 'na-' indicates a long /aː/ vowel, while '-lijk' is a common suffix indicating the adverb form.

eigenlijkactuallymogelijkpossiblewaarschijnlijkprobably

In connected speech

In rapid speech, 'namelijk' can be pronounced with a reduced vowel sound in the suffix, often sounding like /ˈnaːməˌlək/.
