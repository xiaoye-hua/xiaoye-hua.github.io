aanrecht

countertop

/ˈaːnrɛxt/noun

Word forms

  • plural: aanrechten (counters)
  • diminutive: aanrechtje (little counter)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable 'aan' shows a long /aː/, while the closed 'recht' has a short /ɛ/.
