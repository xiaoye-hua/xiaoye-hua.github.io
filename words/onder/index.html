under

/ˈɔndər/preposition

Word forms

  • adverbial form: onderaan (at the bottom)
  • adverbial form: onderin (in the bottom of)
  • adjective form: onderste (bottom, lowest)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Short 'o' /ɔ/ in a closed syllable (on-) followed by an unstressed '-der' where 'e' is pronounced as a schwa /ə/.

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the final /r/ often vocalizes or links smoothly to a following vowel, such as in 'onder ons' [ˈɔndəˌrɔns].
