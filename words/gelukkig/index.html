gelukkig

luckily

/ɣəˈlʏkəx/adverb

Word forms

  • comparative: gelukkiger (happier)
  • superlative: gelukkigst (happiest)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'g' at the beginning and end is pronounced with a voiced velar fricative /ɣ/.

gelukluckongelukkigunhappygelukkighappy

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /ɣ/ in 'gelukkig' may become less distinct, leading to a softer pronunciation.
