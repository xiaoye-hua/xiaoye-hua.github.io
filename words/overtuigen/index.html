overtuigen

convince / persuade (completes 'probeert te...')

/ˌoːvərˈtœyɣə(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'ui' represents the diphthong /œy/, and the 'g' between vowels is pronounced as a velar or uvular fricative /ɣ/ or /χ/.

getuigento testify / witnessbuigento bend / bowzuigento suck / vacuum

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /-ən/ of 'overtuigen' is typically reduced to a simple schwa [-ə], and the /v/ often devoiced slightly to [f] by speakers from the western Netherlands.
