dit

this

/dɪt/demonstrative pronoun

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The short vowel /ɪ/ is followed by a voiceless plosive /t/, typical of closed syllables.

witwhitezitsitspitpit

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /t/ in ‘dit is’ can become a flap [ɾ] or be elided entirely.
