How it is spelled and how it sounds

The vowel 'u' in 'dus' is pronounced as the short /ʏ/ sound, common in closed syllables.

busbusmussparrowkuskiss

In connected speech

The /s/ in ‘dus ik’ can be pronounced as a slightly longer [sː] due to assimilation with the following vowel.
