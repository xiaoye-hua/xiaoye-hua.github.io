vertellen

to tell

/vərˈtɛlə(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Prefix ver- is unstressed, causing reduced vowel /ə/; stem vertel- shows short /ɛ/.

verkopento sellverstaanto understandverhuizento move (house)

In connected speech

The /r/ in ‘vertellen we’ may become a tap [ɾ] in fast speech, smoothing the transition to the next word.
