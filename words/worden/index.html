/ˈʋɛrdə(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Short vowel /ɔ/ followed by /r/ and a dental consonant; in the singular forms (word, wordt, werd), final devoicing turns the /d/ or /dt/ into a voiceless /t/ sound.

bordenplateshordenhurdlesgordento gird

In connected speech

In 'word je' (do you become), the final /t/ sound of 'word' and the /j/ of 'je' often coalesce into a palatal [tʃə] or [cə] sound ('wortje').
