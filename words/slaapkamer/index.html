slaapkamer

bedroom

/ˈslaːpˌkaːmər/noun

Word forms

  • article: de slaapkamer (common gender)
  • plural: slaapkamers (bedrooms)
  • diminutive: slaapkamertje (little bedroom)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The long /aː/ sound is spelled with 'aa' in the closed syllable 'slaap-', and with a single 'a' in the open syllable 'ka-'. The unstressed '-er' ending reduces to a schwa plus r (/ər/).

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /r/ of 'slaapkamer' becomes a clear alveolar tap [ɾ] when linking to a following word that begins with a vowel (e.g., 'slaapkamer in').
