spend the night, stay overnight

/ˌoːvərˈnɑxt/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The '-cht' cluster combines the uvular/velar fricative /x/ with a dental stop /t/, preceded by a short vowel /ɑ/ in a closed syllable.

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the final /t/ of 'overnacht' is often dropped when followed by a word starting with a consonant, such as in 'overnacht je' [ˌoːvərˈnɑxjə].
