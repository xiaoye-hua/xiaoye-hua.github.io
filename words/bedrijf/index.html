bedrijf

company

/bəˈdrɛi̯f/noun

Word forms

  • plural: bedrijven (companies)
  • diminutive: bedrijfje (little company)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'ij' digraph represents the diphthong /ɛi/ in standard Dutch pronunciation.

schrijfwriteblijfstaygrijpgrab

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the final /f/ in 'bedrijf' may become a voiced [v] when followed by a vowel.
