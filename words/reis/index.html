reis

trip

/rɛis/noun

Word forms

  • plural: reizen (journeys, trips)
  • diminutive: reisje (little trip)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diphthong 'ei' is pronounced as /ɛi/.

ijsicewijswiseprijsprice

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the diphthong /ɛi/ in 'reis' may be pronounced more quickly, causing it to sound closer to a single vowel.
