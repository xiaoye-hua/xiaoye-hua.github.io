verbaasd

surprised

/vɛrˈbaːst/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: verbaasder (more surprised)
  • superlative: verbaasdste (most surprised)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'ver-' is unstressed, and the long /aː/ is denoted by 'aa' in a closed syllable.

vermoeidtiredverwardconfusedverlegenshy

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /d/ in 'verbaasd' may become devoiced to [t] when followed by a voiceless consonant.
