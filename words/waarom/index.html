waarom

why

/ʋaːˈrɔm/adverb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable ‘wa-’ shows a long /aː/ sound, while ‘-rom’ has a short /ɔ/.

waarwherewanneerwhenwatwhat

In connected speech

In fast speech, the final /m/ in ‘waarom ik’ can assimilate with a following vowel, sometimes sounding like [ʋaːˈrɔmɪk].
