sollicitatiegesprek

job interview

/sɔˌlɪsɪˈtaː(t)siɣəˌsprɛk/noun

Word forms

  • plural: sollicitatiegesprekken (job interviews)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The double 'l' in 'sollicitatie' indicates a short vowel /ɔ/ in the open syllable. The 'g' in 'gesprek' is pronounced as the voiced velar fricative /ɣ/.

conversatieconversationgesprektalksollicitantapplicant

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /ɣ/ in ‘gesprek’ may become more voiced and lenited, sounding closer to a /x/.
