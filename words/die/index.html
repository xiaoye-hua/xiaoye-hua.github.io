die

who

/di/relative pronoun

How it is spelled and how it sounds

In Dutch, the pronoun 'die' is pronounced with a long /i/ sound, which is common for pronouns and demonstratives.

wiewhohijhezijshe, they

In connected speech

In connected speech, 'die' may become a reduced vowel sound, especially in fast speech or informal contexts.
