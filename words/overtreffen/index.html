overtreffen

beats, surpasses, exceeds

/ˌoːvərˈtrɛft/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Double consonant 'ff' keeps the preceding vowel 'e' short /ɛ/ in open syllables (overtreffen), while a single 'f' suffices at the end of a closed syllable (overtref, overtreft).

treffento hit / to meetbeseffento realizeblaffento bark

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /t/ of 'overtreft' is often dropped before words starting with a consonant (e.g., 'hij overtreft zichzelf' sounds like /ˌoːvərˈtrɛf sɪxˈzɛlf/).
