paar

few / couple (combines with 'een' to mean 'a few')

/paːr/noun

Word forms

  • article: het paar (the pair/couple)
  • plural: paren (pairs, couples)
  • diminutive: paartje (little pair, romantic couple)
  • diminutive plural: paartjes (couples)
  • indefinite usage: een paar (a few / a couple of)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Double 'aa' in a closed syllable represents the long vowel /aː/. In the plural 'paren', the syllable 'pa-' becomes open, requiring only a single 'a' to keep the same long sound.

haarhair / herjaaryearwaartrue / where

In connected speech

In the common phrase 'een paar' (a few), the /n/ of 'een' often assimilates to the following /p/, sounding like [əm ˈpaːr], while the final /r/ is frequently vocalized to [ɐ] at the end of a phrase.
