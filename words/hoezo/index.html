hoezo

how so? / what do you mean? / why?

/ɦuˈzoː/adverb

Word forms

  • invariable: hoezo (no inflected forms)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'oe' represents the short high back vowel /u/, while the final 'o' in an open syllable is pronounced as a long /oː/ with primary stress.

hoehowzoso / like thathoewelalthough

In connected speech

When used as a standalone question expressing surprise or skepticism, the final /oː/ is often significantly lengthened with a sharp rising-falling intonation, and the /z/ may partially devoice to [s].
