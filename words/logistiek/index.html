logistiek

logistics

/loːɣɪˈstik/noun

Word forms

  • plural: logistieken (logistics)
  • diminutive: logistiekje (little logistics)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'g' in 'logistiek' is pronounced as a voiced velar fricative /ɣ/, common in Dutch.

In connected speech

In fast speech, the /k/ at the end of 'logistiek' may become unreleased before a following consonant.
