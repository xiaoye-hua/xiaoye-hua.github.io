Bart

/bɑrt/proper noun

Word forms

  • plural: baarden (beards)
  • diminutive: baardje (little beard)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'aa' in open syllable becomes /aː/, while closed syllable shortens to /ɑ/.

In connected speech, the final /t/ in ‘bart’ can become unreleased or assimilate to a following consonant.
