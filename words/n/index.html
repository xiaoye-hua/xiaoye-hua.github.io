één

one

/eːn/numeral

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The acute accent on 'é' indicates stress and the long vowel /eː/.

éénmaalonceéénsonce (adverb)éénhoornunicorn

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the long vowel /eː/ may slightly shorten, especially before a consonant.
