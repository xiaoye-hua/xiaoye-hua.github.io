baan

job

/baːn/noun

Word forms

  • plural: banen (jobs)
  • diminutive: baantje (little job)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The long /aː/ in the open syllable is maintained in the plural form.

maanmoonraamwindowslaanto hit

In connected speech

In fast speech, the final /n/ in ‘baan is’ may be elided, resulting in a smoother transition between words.
