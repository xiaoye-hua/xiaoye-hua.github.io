video

/ˈvidijoː/noun

Word forms

  • article: de video (masculine/feminine)
  • plural: video's (videos)
  • diminutive: videootje (little video)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Nouns ending in a single long vowel like -o take an apostrophe before the plural -s (video's) to keep the vowel sound long; in the diminutive, the vowel is doubled (videootje) for the same reason.

In connected speech

In connected speech, the initial /v/ often devoices to approaches [f] after a voiceless consonant, and a clear [j] glide is inserted between the 'e' and 'o' sounds.
