omgeving

environment

/ɔmˈɣeːvɪŋ/noun

Word forms

  • plural: omgevingen (environments)
  • diminutive: omgevinkje (little environment)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix om- is unstressed, while -geving has primary stress with a long /eː/ due to the open syllable.

bewegingmovementvergevingforgivenessuitgevingpublication

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /ŋ/ in 'omgeving' may be realized as a more palatalized [ŋj] before front vowels.
