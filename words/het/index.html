het

the

/hɛt/article

Word forms

  • nominative: het (it)
  • accusative: het (it)
  • genitive: zijn (its)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The ‘h’ is often silent in casual speech, making it sound like ‘et’.

metwithzetsetvetfat

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the initial /h/ in ‘het’ may be elided, resulting in a pronunciation closer to [ɛt].
