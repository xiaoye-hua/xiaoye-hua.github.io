How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'ij' represents the diphthong /ɛi̯/. Thevoiced fricative /z/ in 'wijzen' devoicesto /s/ at the end of a syllable, which is also reflected in the spelling ('wijs', 'wijst').

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /t/ in 'wijst' is often dropped when followed by a word starting with a consonant (e.g., 'hij wijst naar' sounds like [ɦɛi̯ ˈʋɛi̯s naːr]).
