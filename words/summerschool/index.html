Summerschool

/ˈsʌmərskuːl/noun

Word forms

  • plural: summerschoolen (summer schools)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The compound 'summerschool' combines 'summer' and 'school', with stress on the first syllable and a long /u/ in 'school'.

zomerschoolsummer schooltaalschoollanguage schoolmuziekschoolmusic school

In connected speech

In connected speech, the /r/ in 'summerschool' may not be fully pronounced, blending smoothly into the following /s/ sound.
