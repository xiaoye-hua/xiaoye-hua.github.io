alvast

already

/ɑlˈvɑst/adverb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'al-' indicates a meaning similar to 'already', with '-vast' implying firmness or certainty.

altijdalwaysalweeragainallichtof course

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /t/ in 'alvast' may be unreleased or assimilated when followed by a consonant.
