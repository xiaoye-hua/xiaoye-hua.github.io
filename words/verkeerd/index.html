verkeerd

wrong / wrongly

/vərˈkeːrt/adverb

Word forms

  • inflected: verkeerde (wrong)
  • comparative: verkeerder (more wrong)
  • superlative: verkeerdst (most wrong)
  • partitive: verkeerds (something wrong, e.g., iets verkeerds)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The unstressed prefix 'ver-' contains a schwa /ə/, the double 'ee' before 'r' produces a long /eː/ (or centering diphthong [ɪː]), and the final 'd' is devoiced to /t/ at the end of the word.

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /t/ (spelled 'd') in 'verkeerd' is often re-voiced to [d] when followed immediately by a word starting with a vowel, such as in 'verkeerd antwoord' [vərˈkeːrd ˈɑntʋoːrt].
