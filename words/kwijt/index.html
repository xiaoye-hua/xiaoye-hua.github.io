kwijt

lost, gone (adjective) — iemand kwijt willen = to lose someone

/kʋɛit/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: kwijter (more lost)
  • superlative: kwijtst (most lost)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diphthong /ɛi/ is commonly spelled as 'ij' in Dutch.

vrijfreeblijhappyrijkrich

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /t/ in ‘kwijt ik’ may assimilate and be pronounced as a glottal stop before a vowel.
