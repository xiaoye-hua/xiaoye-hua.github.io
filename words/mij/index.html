mij

me

/mə/pronoun

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diphthong /ɛi̯/ is common in Dutch pronouns, giving a distinct sound.

zijshe/theywijwejijyou (singular informal)

In connected speech

The word 'mij' may become reduced to a schwa [mə] in fast, connected speech.
