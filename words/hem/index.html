hem

him

/ɦɛm/pronoun

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'h' is pronounced as /ɦ/ and the 'e' as short /ɛ/ in this monosyllabic word.

hemelheavenhemelbedcanopy bedhemelvaartascension

In connected speech

In connected speech, the /ɦ/ in 'hem' may be elided, making it sound like 'em' before certain consonant sounds.
