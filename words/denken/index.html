thinks

/dɛŋkt/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'nk' in 'denk' represents a nasal sound, often pronounced with a more nasal quality.

In connected speech

In fast speech, the 't' in ‘denkt hij’ may assimilate and be pronounced like a softer [d] sound.
