vriendin

girlfriend

/vrinˈdɪn/noun

Word forms

  • gender: de-word (feminine)
  • plural: vriendinnen (female friends / girlfriends)
  • diminutive: vriendinnetje (little female friend / girlfriend)
  • male equivalent: vriend (male friend / boyfriend)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The feminine suffix '-in' always attracts the primary word stress (/ˈɪn/), shifting it away from the root 'vriend'. In the plural 'vriendinnen', the 'n' is doubled to keep the short /ɪ/ vowel in a closed syllable.

koninginqueenleeuwinlionessboerinfemale farmer

In connected speech

In connected speech, the initial voiced /v/ often devoiced to [f] when preceded by a voiceless consonant, such as in 'dat is mijn vriendin'.
