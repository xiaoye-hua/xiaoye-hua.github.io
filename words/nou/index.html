nou

well

/nʌu̯/interjection

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diphthong 'ou' is pronounced as /ʌu/ in Dutch, often found in interjections.

autcar (informal)houwhold (archaic)koudcold

In connected speech

In fast speech, ‘nou’ can be reduced to a shorter, less distinct vowel sound.
