verdrietig

sad

/vərˈdritəx/adjective

Word forms

  • inflected: verdrietige (sad)
  • comparative: verdrietiger (sadder)
  • superlative: verdrietigst (saddest)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The unstressed prefix 'ver-' and adjectival suffix '-ig' both reduce their vowels to the schwa /ə/, while the stressed 'ie' represents a clear /i/ sound.

toevalligcoincidentalgezelligcozy, convivialverstandigsensible, wise

In connected speech

In rapid connected speech, the initial /v/ of 'verdrietig' often devoices to [f] when following a voiceless consonant, such as in 'best verdrietig'.
