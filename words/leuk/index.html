leuk

nice

/løːk/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: leuker (nicer)
  • superlative: leukst (nicest)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The diphthong 'eu' in Dutch generally corresponds to the sound /øː/ or /œy/.

In fast speech, the final /k/ in 'leuk' may be unreleased or assimilate to the following sound.
