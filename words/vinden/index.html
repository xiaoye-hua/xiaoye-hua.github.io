/vɪnt/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Short /ɪ/ in closed syllable vin- indicates the short vowel sound.

bindento bindzendento sendrindento run (archaic)

In connected speech

The final /t/ in ‘vindt hij’ can assimilate to a glottal stop [ʔ] before a vowel.
