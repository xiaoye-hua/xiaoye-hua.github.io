komend

coming / next

/ˈkoːməndə/adjective (present participle)

Word forms

  • base form: komend (coming)
  • inflected form: komende (coming - used before de-words and plural nouns)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable 'ko-' has a long /oː/ sound written with a single 'o', while the unstressed syllables '-men-' and '-de' both reduce their vowels to a schwa /ə/.

lopendewalking / ongoingwonenderesiding / livingkokendeboiling / cooking

In connected speech

In natural, rapid speech, the 'n' in the middle syllable is often dropped or nasalized, and the final schwa may elide before a following vowel, making 'komende' sound like [ˈkoːmədə].
