/ɣəˈbukt/verb (past participle)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'oe' consistently represents the high back rounded vowel /u/ (like 'oo' in English 'boot'), regardless of whether the syllable is open or closed.

In connected speech

In 'geboekt', the prefix 'ge-' is unstressed with a schwa /ɣəˈbukt/, and the final /t/ is often dropped in rapid speech if the next word starts with a consonant.
