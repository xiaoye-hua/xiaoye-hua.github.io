huisje

cabin

/ˈɦœy̯ʃə/noun

Word forms

  • plural: huisjes (little houses)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'ui' diphthong in Dutch is pronounced /œy̯/, and 'je' indicates a diminutive form, pronounced /jə/.

huishousetuinhuisjegarden shedhuisraadhousehold goods

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /j/ in 'huisje' might assimilate slightly to a softer sound depending on the preceding word, linking smoothly into the next vowel.
