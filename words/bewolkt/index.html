bewolkt

cloudy / overcast

/bəˈʋɔlkt/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: bewolkter (cloudier)
  • superlative: het meest bewolkt (the most cloudy)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix be- is unstressed and pronounced as /bə/, while the closed syllable -wolkt maintains a short /ɔ/ vowel.

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /t/ of 'bewolkt' may be assimilated or unreleased, especially before a voiced consonant.
