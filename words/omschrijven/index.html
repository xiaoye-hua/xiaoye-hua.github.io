omschrijven

describe / am describing

/ɔmˈsxrɛif/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The consonant cluster 'schr-' is pronounced /sxr/, combining the voiceless velar fricative /x/ with the /r/, while the 'v' devoiced to 'f' at the end of a syllable.

schrijvento writebeschrijvento describeschrikkento be startled

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final '-n' of 'omschrijven' is usually dropped, and the final /f/ in 'omschrijf het' may voice slightly to [v] before the vowel of 'het' (pronounced 't).
