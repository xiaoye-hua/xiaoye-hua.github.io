avond

evening

/ˈaːvɔnt/noun

Word forms

  • plural: avonden (evenings)
  • diminutive: avondje (little evening)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open syllable a- indicates a long vowel /aː/, which remains consistent in pronunciation.

avondmaalsupperavondklokcurfewavondroodsunset glow

In connected speech

The final /t/ in ‘avond’ often becomes unreleased or assimilates to a glottal stop in connected speech.
