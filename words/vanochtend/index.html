vanochtend

this morning

/vɑnˈɔxtənt/adverb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'van-' is a common prefix meaning 'of' or 'from' and is unstressed, while 'ochtend' is stressed.

vanmiddagthis afternoonvannachttonightvandaagtoday

In connected speech

The /n/ in 'vanochtend' may assimilate to a nasalized [ŋ] before the velar /x/ in connected speech.
