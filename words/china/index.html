china

China

/ˈʃinaː/proper noun

Word forms

  • plural: china's (china)
  • diminutive: chineesje (little china)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'ch' in Dutch can be pronounced as /ʃ/ before 'i'.

chicchicchimpanseechimpanzeechloorchlorine

In connected speech

The initial /ʃ/ in ‘china’ may become more sibilant in fast speech, making it sound closer to /sʃ/.
