voor

for

/voːr/preposition

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The long vowel /oː/ in 'voor' is due to the open syllable, which is typical in Dutch pronunciation patterns.

doorthroughhoorhear (1sg present)koorchoir

In connected speech

In connected speech, the /r/ in 'voor' may be pronounced as a tap [ɾ] or even elided entirely, depending on the speaker and regional accent.
