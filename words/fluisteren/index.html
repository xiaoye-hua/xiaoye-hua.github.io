fluisteren

whispers

/ˈflœystərt/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'ui' represents the diphthong /œy̯/, while the unstressed '-er-' and '-en' syllables reduce to a schwa /ə/ sound.

luisterento listenduisterdark / gloomyfluitento whistle

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final '-n' of 'fluisteren' is usually dropped (/ˈflœy̯stərə/), and in 'fluistert hij', the /t/ and /ɦ/ merge so it sounds like /ˈflœy̯stərtie/.
