How it is spelled and how it sounds

In Dutch, 'al' is pronounced with a short /ɑ/ sound, and 's' retains its /s/ sound.

In connected speech

In connected speech, the /s/ in 'als' can become voiced to [z] when followed by a voiced consonant.
