also

/oːk/adverb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The long vowel /oː/ is indicated by the open syllable followed by a consonant.

rooksmokekookcook (singular noun)lookleek

In connected speech

In casual speech, the vowel /oː/ can sometimes become shorter before a following consonant.
