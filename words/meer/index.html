meer

more

/meːr/adverb

Word forms

  • plural: meren (lakes)
  • diminutive: meertje (little lake)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The spelling 'ee' in 'meer' indicates a long vowel sound /eː/.

beerbearveerfeatherzeervery

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /r/ in 'meer' might be pronounced as a more approximant sound or even dropped, depending on the regional accent.
