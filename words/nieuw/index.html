nieuw

new

/ˈniwə/adjective

Word forms

  • inflected: nieuwe (new)
  • comparative: nieuwer (newer)
  • superlative: nieuwst (newest)
  • partitive: nieuws (something new / news)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The combination -ieuw represents the diphthong /iu/ (or /iyw/), where the 'e' is silent in pronunciation.

kieuwgillbenieuwdcuriousopnieuwagain / anew

In connected speech

In the inflected form 'nieuwe', the 'w' acts as a bilabial glide [ʋ] or [w] linking to the final schwa, which is often elided before a word starting with a vowel (e.g., 'nieuwe auto' sounds like [ˈniʋ ˈɔutoː]).
