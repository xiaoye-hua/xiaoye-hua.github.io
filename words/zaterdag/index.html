zaterdag

Saturday

/ˈzaːtərdɑx/noun

Word forms

  • gender: de zaterdag (masculine)
  • plural: zaterdagen (Saturdays)
  • diminutive: zaterdagje (little Saturday)
  • adverbial: zaterdags (on Saturdays)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The open first syllable 'za-' represents a long /aː/ with a single 'a'. The final 'g' is devoiced to /x/ at the end of the word, while the plural 'zaterdagen' lengthens the second 'a' to /aː/ as the syllable becomes open (da-gen).

In connected speech

In Northern Dutch speech, the initial /z/ is frequently devoiced to [s] (sounding like 'saterdag'), and the 'r' before 'd' may be weakly articulated or realized as an approximant [ɹ].
