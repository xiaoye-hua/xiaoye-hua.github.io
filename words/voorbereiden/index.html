prepare

/ˈvoːr.bəˌrɛi̯.də(n)/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The prefix 'voor-' has a long /oː/ vowel, typical of open syllables.

voorspellento predictvoorstellento proposevoorzichtigcareful

In connected speech

In rapid speech, the /d/ in 'voorbereid ik' may assimilate to a flap or be dropped before a vowel.
