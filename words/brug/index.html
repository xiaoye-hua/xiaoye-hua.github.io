brug

little bridge

/ˈbrʏɣə(c)çə/noun

Word forms

  • singular: de brug (the bridge)
  • plural: bruggen (bridges)
  • diminutive: het bruggetje (the little bridge)
  • diminutive plural: bruggetjes (little bridges)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Nouns with a short vowel followed by a velar fricative (-g) take '-etje' in the diminutive, and the consonant is doubled ('gg') to keep the preceding 'u' sound short /ʏ/.

vlaggetjelittle flagweggetjelittle roadmuggetjelittle mosquito

In connected speech

In natural speech, the '-tje' cluster in 'bruggetje' is frequently palatalized to sound like [tʃə] or [cə], and the final schwa may elide before a word starting with a vowel.
