proberen

tries / is trying (takes a 'te + infinitive' clause: te overtuigen)

/proːˈbeːrt/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Verbs of French origin ending in unstressed '-eren' carry the stress on the penultimate syllable (/beː/). In open syllables (pro-be-ren) the long vowel is written with one 'e', while in closed syllables (pro-beer-t) it requires 'ee'.

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /t/ in 'probeert' is often dropped when followed by a word starting with a consonant (e.g., 'hij probeert dat' sounds like /proːˈbeːr dɑt/), and the /n/ in 'proberen' is typically elided to /proːˈbeːrə/.
