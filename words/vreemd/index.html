vreemd

strange

/vreːmt/adjective

Word forms

  • inflected: vreemde (strange)
  • comparative: vreemder (stranger)
  • superlative: vreemdst (strangest)
  • partitive: vreemds (something strange)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Double 'ee' in a closed syllable represents the long vowel /eː/, and the final 'd' is devoiced to /t/ at the end of the word.

breedwidekleedrug / garmentleedsuffering / grief

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /t/ sound of 'vreemd' may become voiced to [d] before avoiced stop (like 'b' or 'd') or may be dropped entirely in casual speech between consonants, sounding like [vreːm].
