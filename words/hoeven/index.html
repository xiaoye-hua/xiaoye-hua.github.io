hoeven

needed (past of hoeven; with niet … te: didn't have to)

/ˈɦuvdə/verb

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The digraph 'oe' represents the close back rounded vowel /u/ (like 'oo' in English 'boot'), while the 'v' shifts to 'f' at the end of a syllable due to final obstruent devoicing.

boevencrooks / thievesproevento tasteschroevento screw / screws

In connected speech

In the past tense form 'hoefde', the /f/ often voices to [v] before the voiced /d/ due to regressive assimilation, sounding like [ˈɦuvdə].
