stil

quiet

/stɪl/adjective

Word forms

  • comparative: stiller (quieter)
  • superlative: stilst (quietest)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Short vowel /ɪ/ in closed syllable ‘-il’ keeps the vowel short.

stiltesilencestillevenstill lifestilstaanto stand still

In connected speech

The final /l/ in ‘stil’ may be slightly velarized when followed by a vowel, as in ‘stil is’.
