alles

everything (direct object of 'overtreft')

/ˈɑləs/indefinite pronoun

Word forms

  • base adjective/determiner: al (all)
  • inflected form: alle (all)
  • plural pronoun (people): allen (everyone, all)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

Double 'll' keeps the preceding 'a' short (/ɑ/), while the unstressed 'e' in the final syllable reduces to a schwa (/ə/).

vallento fallballenballshallenhalls

In connected speech

In connected speech, the final /s/ often voices to [z] before a word starting with a vowel or voiced consonant, as in 'alles is' ([ˈɑləz ɪs]).
