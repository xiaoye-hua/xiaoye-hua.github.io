een

a

/eːn/article

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The article ‘een’ is pronounced with a long /eː/ to distinguish it from the numeral ‘één’, which has a stressed pronunciation.

éénonegeenno, nonenegennine

In connected speech

In rapid speech, ‘een’ may be reduced to a schwa sound /ən/ when it is unstressed.
