verder

further

/ˈvɛrdər/adverb

Word forms

  • comparative: verder (further)
  • superlative: verst (furthest)

How it is spelled and how it sounds

The 'er' ending is pronounced /ər/, a common pattern in Dutch adjectives.

anderotherbeterbettervaderfather

In connected speech

In casual speech, the /r/ at the end of ‘verder’ may be softer or even drop out, resulting in a pronunciation closer to [ˈvɛdə].
