Notes

Error-born (2026-09-17 recite). hua invented a verb, drinkeren, reaching for English "drink". Dutch puts the drinking in a noun + the particle op: drank op hebben = to have alcohol inside you. op goes to the end: Hij heeft er twee op. Same particle instinct as the separable verbs — the unit is the chunk, not a verb.
