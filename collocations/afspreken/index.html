No clean English equivalent — covers "make plans", "arrange to meet", "agree on". Separable: ik spreek af, we hebben afgesproken. Noun een afspraak (appointment) is the high-value spin-off. English speakers reach for ontmoeten/"meet" here; Dutch uses afspreken. 2026-09-09 → produced (s8): hua wrote "we moeten afspreken" — correct verb, correct infinitive form, correctly at the end of the clause. They did not reach for ontmoeten, which was the predicted English-speaker error. The sentence failed on the surrounding dan-inversion and the missing want, not on this chunk.
