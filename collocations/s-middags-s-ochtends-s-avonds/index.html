Notes

Error-born (2026-09-19 recite, s45). hua built it from parts — in de dag — which is not Dutch. These are frozen forms with an apostrophe-s (a fossil genitive): 's ochtends, 's middags, 's avonds, 's nachts. Not in de …, and note 's nachts is irregular against de nacht.
