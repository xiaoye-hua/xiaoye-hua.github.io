Word-by-Word Practice (18 sentences)

Daarom zegt ze tegen Marieke: "Ik ben best wel benieuwd hoe het met jou en Gianluca gaat."

That's why she says to Marieke: "I'm quite curious how things are going with you and Gianluca."

New word

best = quite · Pronunciation: /bɛst/

wel = indeed · Pronunciation: /ʋɛl/

Active Recall Practice

  • Prompt: Say 'quite' → best
  • Review: Say 'Xing, Annika and Marieke are sitting together in cabin 65.' → Xing, Annika en Marieke zitten bij elkaar in huisje 65.
  • Prompt: Say 'indeed' → wel
  • Review: Say 'quite' → best

Full Sentence Practice
