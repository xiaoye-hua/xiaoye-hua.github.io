HOW I STUDY

Material: the “Heb je zin?” video lessons from learndutch.org.

1 lesson = 2 parts · 1 part = 2 days

Day 1
Morning Go through every sentence of the part and mark the words and sentences I don't know. A Pimsleur audio is made from my marks. Daytime Listen to the Pimsleur audio. Evening Recite #1: write the Dutch from the English, and note the problems.
Day 2
Morning Recite #2 and note the problems. The sentences I still don't know become Anki cards. Daytime Listen to the Pimsleur audio again.
Day 3
The next part.
Every day
Anki review.
