Which verb FORM belongs in which slot (conjugated / stem / infinitive)

#dutchgrammar les 28 en 29 en 41

Amodal verb + infinitive at the end les 28 · 11 sentences

willen, kunnen, mogen, moeten (and zullen) are irregular: ik/jij/hij wil · kan · mag · moet, wij/jullie/zij willen · kunnen · mogen · moeten. The modal is the finite verb; the infinitive goes to the END of the sentence, and niet goes right before that infinitive. Question with jij: kan/kun jij? — with u: kunt u? / wilt u?
