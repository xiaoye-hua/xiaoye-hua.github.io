Past tense or present perfect? (imperfectum vs perfectum)

#dutchgrammar-2 les 22

Bpast tense or present perfect? les 22 · 5 sentences

Don't pick the tense the way English would — Bart's slide says it in bold. Present perfect (heeft gezegd) = a single event, the result, a plain fact, fine without a time. Past tense (zei, kreeg, kon) = a story or sequence (en toen? daarna …), a habit, a process, and only with a time indication (gisteren, vanavond).
