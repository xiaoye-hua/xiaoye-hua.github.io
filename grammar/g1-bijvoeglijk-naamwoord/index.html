Dspelling when -e is added les 10 · 2 sentences

Adding -e opens the syllable, so the spelling adjusts: short vowel doubles the consonant, a double long vowel loses a letter, f→v, s→z; -ig and diphthongs don't change.

Funcountable het-word: no -e les 17 · 1 sentence

An uncountable het-word (water, bier, geld) with no article — or after veel, meer, weinig, minder — is indefinite, so no -e. Uncountable de-words still get -e.
