Ate + adjective = too les 18 · 4 sentences

te right before an adjective or adverb means 'too'. Don't confuse it with te before an infinitive (te lezen, om … te vertellen), which just marks the verb.

Biets / wat / niets … + adjective + s les 18 · 5 sentences

After a quantity word — iets, wat, niets, veel, weinig, genoeg, allerlei, meer, minder, voldoende — an adjective with no noun after it gets -s. English says 'something nice'; Dutch says iets leuks.

Cdie + adjective-e, noun left out les 18 · 1 sentence

When the noun is already clear, Dutch can drop it and keep the adjective with its -e: Welke appel? Die grote. Bart's advice on the slide: when you speak, just add the noun.
